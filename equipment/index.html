Equipment

The things that are physically out there, and what you need to know about each one to be useful in a conversation about it.

One of these is finished. The rest are listed so you know what's coming and can tell at a glance that it isn't here yet.

  • Pumps The pump curve, NPSH, and what sets the pressure ceiling. If you only have time for one fluids topic, make it this one.
  • Control valves Cv, valve characteristics, and why a valve is either controlling something or it is just a restriction.
  • Heat exchangers Shell and tube arrangements, approach temperature, and how fouling quietly eats your performance.
  • Distillation columns Reflux ratio, trays versus packing, flooding, and what the temperature profile is telling you.
  • Tanks and vessels Level measurement, blanketing, breathing losses, and the failure modes nobody warns you about.
  • Compressors Centrifugal versus reciprocating, surge, and why compressors are harder to live with than pumps.
  • Fired heaters Draft, tube skin temperatures, coking, and the interlocks that exist because of past incidents.
  • Piping and hydraulics Line sizing, pressure drop, two-phase flow, and building the system curve a pump has to work against.
  • Relief systems and PSVs Overpressure scenarios, set pressure versus MAWP, and why a relief calculation is effectively a legal document.
  • Filters and strainers Differential pressure as a diagnostic, changeout criteria, and what plugging does to everything upstream.
  • Steam systems Headers, traps, condensate return, and why steam quality turns up in problems that look unrelated.
  • Instrumentation basics What a transmitter is actually measuring, why it drifts, and when not to believe one.

Written by one process engineer at a refinery. Corrections welcome.
